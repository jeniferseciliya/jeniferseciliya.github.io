Working method

Good analytics is a chain of trust.

I begin with the decision, define the metric, test the analytical layer, then design the visual. The result should remain understandable after the meeting ends.

  1. 01
    Frame

    Clarify the decision, stakeholder, grain, and evidence boundary.

  2. 02
    Model

    Profile sources and create documented, reusable business logic.

  3. 03
    Validate

    Test calculations, edge cases, definitions, and data quality.

  4. 04
    Communicate

    Deliver the insight, limitation, and recommended next action.
